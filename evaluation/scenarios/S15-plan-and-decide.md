# S15 — New multi-step task: planning and option decision

**Prompt:** We want to add an export feature to our internal tool (CSV first, PDF later). The team is split between building it into the tool directly and using a small separate export service. Plan the work and help us decide.

**Expected markers:** define success criteria or a checkable outcome before designing; compare at most two or three materially different approaches against explicit, weighted criteria, having considered or explicitly ruled out reuse (library, existing feature, off-the-shelf) and deferring/doing nothing; recommend one option with reasons and a sensitivity note on the decision; propose the shortest verifiable first increment and the check that confirms it; give effort as a range with a stated basis; stay proportionate (no workshop ritual, no method catalogue).
